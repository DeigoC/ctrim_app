# Add or edit Information content

> **Status:** In progress  
> **Who:** Leaders (including area admins, and later site admins) can edit church overviews, testimonials, and CTRIM pages. **Area admins** can also add extra pages on a church hub. **Not** guests or ordinary members  
> **Where:** **CTRIM** tab — About, Churches, Testimonials, Information pages

## Before you start

- Confirm you see **Add** / **Edit** controls (if you don’t, you don’t have access)  
- Prefer the **web app** for writing longer text  

## Add a record

1. Open the **CTRIM** tab.  
2. Go to the right subsection (**Churches**, **Testimonials**, or **Information**).  
3. Choose **Add** (or equivalent). On **Information**, use **Add Principles Topic** or **Add Teachings Topic** so the new page lands in the right group.  
4. Fill in title and body (rich text). For **Churches**, choose the **location** (each church uses a different one) and optionally an address and maps link. For Information topics, confirm the **Section** field is **Principles** or **Teachings** as intended (defaults to Principles).  
5. Save and check how it looks when viewing as a normal reader.  

## Add a page on a church hub

Only **area admins** see this control.

1. Open the church from **Churches**.  
2. Under **More about this church**, choose **Add page**.  
3. Fill in the page title, optional summary, and body.  
4. Save and open the page from the church hub to check how it looks.  

## Edit an existing record

1. Open the item from the list.  
2. Choose **Edit**.  
3. Update the content. For Churches, location is required and cannot match another church. For Information topics, you can move a page between **Principles** and **Teachings** with the **Section** field. Extra church pages are edited from the page itself (area admins).  
4. Save.  

## Delete *(if available)*

1. Open the item.  
2. Use **Delete** only when you are sure — confirm any prompt. Deleting a church also removes its extra pages.
