# Register or edit people

> **Status:** In progress  
> **Who:** Leaders (people they work with), area admins (area scope), later site admins  
> **Where:** Personal → **Volunteers** / register & edit flows

## Before you start

- Know whether you are registering a full person or a **placeholder** (someone needed on a roster before they sign in) — see [People & roles](../../people-and-roles.md)  
- Prefer the **web app** for forms with many fields  

## Register someone

1. Open **Personal**.  
2. Open **Volunteers** (or the register-user entry point available to you).  
3. Choose **Register** / **Add**.  
4. Enter name and other required details (location, tags, roles as applicable).  
5. Save.  
6. Confirm they appear in the directory / can be picked on posts.  

## Edit someone

1. Find the person in **Volunteers** (search / filters by location or tags).  
2. Open their profile.  
3. **Edit** the fields you need.  
4. Save.  

## Placeholders

1. When assigning someone who isn’t in the directory yet, use **create placeholder** (wording may vary) from the people picker.  
2. Fill in at least a name so the roster stays clear.  
3. Later, when they sign in, their account can be linked — describe that flow here once screenshots exist.
