# Register or edit people

> **Status:** In progress  
> **Who:** Leaders (people they work with), area admins (area scope), later site admins  
> **Where:** Personal → **People** → **Who's who** / register & edit flows

## Before you start

- Know whether you are registering a full person or a **placeholder** (someone needed on a roster before they sign in) — see [People & roles](../../people-and-roles.md)  
- Prefer the **web app** for forms with many fields  

## Register someone

1. Open **Personal**.  
2. Open **Who's who** (under **People**). The list starts on people who **serve** at your location — turn **Serving** off in **Refine list** to see everyone.  
3. Choose **Register** / **Add**.  
4. Enter name and other required details (location, tags, roles as applicable).  
5. Save.  
6. Confirm they appear in the directory / can be picked on posts.  

## Edit someone

1. Find the person in **Who's who** (search / filters by location, Serving, or tags).  
2. Open their profile.  
3. **Edit** the fields you need.  
4. Save.  

## Placeholders

1. Open the people picker (for example **Manage members** on a programme role, attendance, or cell members). Existing placeholders appear in the list; use the **Placeholders** chip to show only those profiles.  
2. If they are not listed yet, search for their name; when nothing matches, tap **Create placeholder**.  
3. Fill in forename and surname. The new profile is created and selected so you can assign them immediately.  
4. Later, when they sign in, their account can be linked — describe that flow here once screenshots exist.
