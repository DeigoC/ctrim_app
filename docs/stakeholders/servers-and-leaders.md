# For servers and leaders

A short start if you serve on a team or lead a ministry. The rest of this site goes into each part when you need it. A normal week only needs the first few steps.

!!! tip "Stop when this covers your week"
    Find the post, check the schedule, and know your ministry. Templates, attendance, and admin tools are further down for when you actually need them.

## What the app is for

CTRIM Community is where the church sees **what is on**, and **who is doing it**.

| Piece | In plain terms |
|-------|----------------|
| **Bulletin** | The list of events and meetings. Each one is a **post**. |
| **Schedule** | On that post: the running order, and who is assigned to each part. **My Schedule** (under Personal) is the list of parts assigned to you. |
| **Ministry** | Your team — Worship, Welcome, Technical, and similar. A person can belong to a ministry, and a schedule slot can be marked for one. |
| **Post tag** | A label on the event so people can filter the bulletin (Sunday Worship, Midweek, and similar). |

## If you serve on a team

1. **Sign in**, so the app can show assignments under your name. Personal → **For You** offers sign-in when you are browsing as a guest.
2. Open the **Bulletin** tab and find the post — this Sunday, your midweek, your meeting.
3. Open **Personal** and use **My Schedule**. The card on Personal previews what is coming up. **View Full Schedule** opens the full list of where you are assigned.

That is enough to turn up in the right place.

When you want a bit more:

- **[Ministries](personal.md#people-teams)** — Personal → **People & Teams** → **View Ministries** shows each team, who is in it, and its schedule.
- **[Bookmarks and notifications](notifications.md)** — keep a post on a shortlist, or hear about a location you care about.
- **[Post tags](events-and-bulletin.md#finding-posts-sort-filters)** — narrow the bulletin to one kind of event.

## If you lead a ministry

Do the three steps above. Then open **Ministry Schedule** (Personal → **People & Teams**).

It looks ahead at slots for a ministry, including slots that still need someone. It opens on your location and the ministries you belong to or head. If you head that ministry at that church, you can **assign people** on an upcoming slot from there. The time and the title of the slot stay on the post.

When the running order itself needs to change — a new item, a different time, who is on one event — that lives on the **post**. Open the post from the bulletin or from Ministry Schedule.

**Leaders** can create posts and edit them. The person who created a post, and anyone listed as a contributor, can edit that post too. Step-by-step: [Create a post](how-to/posts/create-a-post.md) · [Edit a post](how-to/posts/edit-a-post.md).

### Ministry and post tag

These two sit near each other and do different jobs.

| | What it is | Where you meet it |
|--|------------|-------------------|
| **Ministry** | The team a person belongs to, and the team a schedule slot can be marked for | A profile, a schedule line, **View Ministries**, **Ministry Schedule** |
| **Post tag** | A label on the event, so the bulletin can be filtered | Bulletin filters, and on the post itself |

A Sunday service might carry the post tag **Sunday Worship**, while the people on the platform are in the **Worship** ministry.
